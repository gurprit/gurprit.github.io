PartySpeaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

PartySpeaker ("the App") allows users to synchronise music playback between multiple Android devices connected to the same local network.

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains what information PartySpeaker collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

PartySpeaker does not require you to create an account.

PartySpeaker does not collect, store or transmit personally identifiable information.

The app does not collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Payment information

Local Network Communication

PartySpeaker communicates directly with other devices connected to the same local Wi-Fi network in order to:

  • Discover nearby PartySpeaker devices
  • Synchronise playlists
  • Transfer music between participating devices
  • Coordinate playback timing

This communication takes place directly between your devices and is never routed through PartySpeaker servers.

Music Files

Music selected by the host device is transferred only to devices participating in the current party session.

  • No music is uploaded to cloud servers.
  • No music is shared outside your local network.
  • Transferred music is used solely for playback.

Data Sharing

PartySpeaker does not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties.

Advertising

PartySpeaker does not display advertisements.

Analytics

PartySpeaker does not use analytics or tracking services to monitor user behaviour.

Security

All communication occurs directly between participating devices on your local network. No user accounts or cloud services are required.

Children's Privacy

PartySpeaker is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.
