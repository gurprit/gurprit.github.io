Party Lord 👑

Privacy policy

For Party Lord, the Party Speaker companion app, and the browser-based guest player.

Effective date: 25 November 2026

In short: Party Lord hosts music playback for nearby devices on the same local network. Party Speaker and browser guests join that session. The apps use information needed to discover and connect devices, load music you select and coordinate playback. Music and session information can be transferred between participants' devices on the local network.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Party Lord (the paid Android hosting/DJ application), Party Speaker (the Android listening companion) and the browser guest player opened from a Party Lord invitation. They are referred to together as the “apps” below. The apps are provided by the independent developer of Party Lord.

2. What information is used

  • Music you choose: Party Lord accesses audio files, filenames, track titles, artists, album artwork and related metadata when you explicitly import or select them from your device or a supported document provider, including Google Drive.
  • Local-network and session details: To connect a host and guests, the apps use local IP addresses, party codes, invitations, temporary session credentials, connection/readiness state, device timing information and playback position.
  • Temporary audio copies: The host and guests may cache selected music, artwork and metadata so playback can be synchronized. The browser may also temporarily buffer media under its normal browser behaviour.
  • Optional Google Drive access: When you choose a track or folder through a Google/Android picker, the app uses the authorization or document-access permission needed to read the items you selected. A chosen document-tree permission may remain available until it is revoked. The app may temporarily cache selected audio for playback.
  • App permissions: Party Lord may request access to selected audio, Wi-Fi/network information and notifications or foreground playback permissions as applicable to your Android version. Party Speaker uses network and background audio capabilities to receive and play a session.

3. How information is used and shared

Information is used to import music, display what is playing, join or host a party, transfer or stream the music selected by the host, estimate timing differences, keep speakers in sync, reconnect and troubleshoot connection errors.

During a party, the host can provide participating Android devices and authorized browser guests with track names, artist/album metadata, artwork, playback state and copies or streams of selected audio over the local network. A person who has been given access to a party may receive this music. Only share music you have the right to use and distribute.

Google Drive, Google Play and your device/browser provider process information under their own privacy policies when you use their services. Purchases of Party Lord are processed by Google Play; the apps do not directly request or store your payment card details.

4. Storage, retention and security

Music selected for a party and temporary session information are processed primarily on the participating devices and the local network, rather than through a developer-operated music server. Temporary cached files may remain on a device until the app clears them, the operating system removes them, you clear app storage or you uninstall the app. Selected document access may persist until revoked in Android or Google account settings.

Party invitations and local session credentials are designed to be temporary. The apps take measures to restrict media requests to participating guests, but local networks and participant devices are not guaranteed secure. Use trusted networks and only invite people you intend to share music with.

5. Analytics, advertising and sale of data

The current apps do not include a developer-operated advertising service or a developer-operated analytics account. We do not sell personal data. Platform providers may collect their own diagnostic and operational information when delivering Android, Google Play, Google Drive or browser services; their terms and privacy policies apply.

6. Your choices and requests

You can decline to import music, cancel a Google Drive picker, stop hosting, disconnect from a party, clear temporary files using available in-app/system storage controls, revoke document permissions and uninstall the apps. You can ask about information associated with use of the apps via the contact method below. We cannot erase information already copied to another participant's device; participants control their own devices and copies.

The apps do not currently offer a developer-hosted account system. If that changes, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

7. Children

The apps are designed for general party and music playback, not specifically for children. They are not intended to request children's personal information. Please contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information directly to the developer through a support channel.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, integrations or relevant requirements change. The effective date will be updated on this page.
