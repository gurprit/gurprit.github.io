Good to know

Questions? Answered.

Do all devices need to use the same Wi-Fi?

Yes. Party Lord hosts the party on the local network and participating Android and browser guests need to reach that host. Some guest Wi-Fi or isolated networks block device-to-device connections.

Does everyone need to buy Party Lord?

No. Only the DJ/host needs Party Lord (£2.99). Friends use Party Speaker free on Android or the browser option when the host enables Web Guests.

Can I use Spotify or other subscription music apps?

Party Lord currently plays music files you select from your device or supported file providers such as Google Drive. It does not import playback directly from Spotify, Apple Music or other streaming subscriptions.

Does it work on iPhones?

The DJ and native Speaker apps are Android products. The browser guest can be opened on other devices, including compatible mobile browsers. iOS and some other browsers may interrupt audio when the screen locks or the app is backgrounded.

Do I need an internet connection?

The synchronized party uses local Wi-Fi. Downloading music from Google Drive, installing the apps and other external services require internet access, but local file playback does not require a developer-hosted music streaming service.

Is Party Lord available on Google Play yet?

Not yet. Party Lord is being prepared for Google Play at £2.99, and the free Party Speaker companion is planned alongside it. Store links will be added when the apps are published.
