PRISM SHIFT

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Prism Shift is an offline puzzle game. No account, advertising, analytics tracking or game-operated cloud service is required. Your game progress and preferences are stored locally.

1. About the game and developer

Prism Shift is an Android optical puzzle game developed by Gurprit Sahota. This policy applies to the production Android application (package name: uk.gurpritsahota.prismshift) and explains its data practices and your privacy choices.

2. Information used by the game

The game stores the following information locally to provide its features:

  • Puzzle and tutorial progress, completed courses and unlocked levels.
  • Course seeds and versions, scores, bonuses, records and replay information.
  • Sound preferences and other game settings, where applicable.

This information is used to resume your games, calculate scores and restore your preferences. The game does not require an account or request your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or payment card details. It does not use advertising identifiers or include analytics or advertising services.

3. Collection, transmission and sharing

Prism Shift does not transmit gameplay saves or preferences to a developer-operated server. It does not sell personal information or share game data with advertisers, analytics providers or data brokers. The puzzles can be played without an internet connection after installation.

If you contact the developer directly, any information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, feedback or screenshots, is used to respond to your enquiry and investigate reported problems. It is not used for advertising. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

4. Google Play, Android and website hosting

Google Play processes purchases, downloads and distribution under its own privacy policy. Prism Shift does not receive your payment card details. Android and Google Play may independently process device, installation or diagnostic information as part of their platform services and your settings.

Depending on your Android backup and device-transfer settings, the operating system may back up or restore some locally stored game information. These services are managed by the platform, not by Prism Shift.

This policy webpage is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. This page contains no advertising, tracking scripts or external font requests.

5. Storage, retention and deletion

Game data is retained on your device until replaced through normal gameplay or removed. To delete it, open Android Settings, find Prism Shift under Apps, then select Storage and Clear data (sometimes labelled Clear storage). This resets your progress and preferences. Uninstalling also removes the app's local data. Platform-managed backups may remain and can be managed through your Android backup settings.

Prism Shift has no server-side user accounts or game data to delete. Any support correspondence is retained only as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, resolve problems or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence by contacting the developer.

6. Security

The game minimises privacy risks by keeping its gameplay information in Android's application storage rather than a central server. Android's application isolation provides platform-level protection. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children's privacy

Prism Shift does not require players to provide personal information or create accounts, and does not knowingly collect children's personal information through gameplay. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, please contact us so we can address the issue.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the game's data practices change. Revisions will be published here with an updated effective date.

9. Privacy contact

For privacy questions, concerns or requests, contact the developer, Gurprit Sahota, at gurprit.sahota@gmail.com.
